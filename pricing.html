Pricing / 05

Clear entry pricing. Written scope before work.

The project-value tiers below are starting fees for a defined bid-preparation engagement. Documents, trade, deadline, requested outputs, and complexity determine the final written proposal.

No paid engagement begins until scope, schedule, deliverables, exclusions, price, and payment terms are defined.

Bid-preparation starting fee

A transparent framework tied to project contract value.

These figures establish the entry tier. They are not automatic totals, package prices, deposits, or guarantees.

Additional support

Scoped only when it adds value.

Post-bid clarification, expanded deliverables, model coordination, or continued support are not assumed to equal the starting fee. Any additional phase is separately described, priced, and authorized in writing.

  • No automatic doubled package price
  • No automatic success fee or discount
  • Defined deliverables and payment trigger
  • Contractor approval before added work

What controls the quote

Price follows the work, not just project value.

Contract value provides an entry tier. Actual effort depends on the document set and the decision required.

DOCUMENTS

Volume and quality

Number of files, disciplines, specifications, addenda, revisions, organization, and missing information.

SCOPE

Trade and complexity

Systems, demolition, interfaces, alternatives, phasing, active-facility conditions, and requested detail.

TIME

Deadline and priority

Available review window, sequencing, response dependencies, and expedited delivery needs.

OUTPUT

Deliverables requested

Quantity summaries, markups, assumption logs, scope-gap review, RFI drafts, pricing support, and working backup.

Commercial clarity

The written proposal controls the engagement.

It identifies source documents, services, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, schedule, price, payment terms, and client responsibilities.

HardBid Consulting does not guarantee bid award, final quantities, final pricing, profit, schedule, code compliance, or project outcome.
