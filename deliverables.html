The work product

Built to be reviewed, corrected, and trusted responsibly.

Deliverables are designed for traceability. They distinguish what the documents confirm from what the team must still verify, price, clarify, or approve.

01

Document Control Index

Drawing, specification, addenda, bid-form, and revision inventory with review status.

02

Quantity Summary

Measured quantities and counts with units, sources, measurement basis, and status.

03

Marked-Up References

Review annotations tied to relevant plans, details, schedules, or specification sections.

04

Assumption Log

Items not fully defined by the documents and the basis used for continued review.

05

Scope-Gap Register

Missing, conflicting, overlapping, or uncertain responsibilities and interfaces.

06

Decision Summary

Open RFIs, quotes, allowances, exclusions, and contractor approvals required before close.

Quality control

What a review-ready package makes visible.

Nothing should appear more certain than the documents allow. HardBid Consulting uses status language to keep confidence and responsibility clear.

SOURCE CONTROL

Where did it come from?

Relevant drawing, detail, schedule, specification, addendum, quote, or contractor direction is recorded wherever practical.

STATUS CONTROL

How certain is it?

Items can be labeled verified, assumed, pending, excluded, or contractor review required.

REVISION CONTROL

Is it current?

The working set and known revisions are identified so superseded information is less likely to drive the number.

DECISION CONTROL

Who must act?

Questions and pricing inputs are routed to the contractor for final direction, approval, or submission.

Important

A deliverable is decision support—not final professional certification.

HardBid Consulting outputs are based on the information provided and the scope authorized. Missing or conflicting documents can affect completeness.

The contractor or client must independently review and approve all quantities, assumptions, exclusions, prices, and bid decisions before use.
