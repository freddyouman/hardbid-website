Services / 01

Review support built around the way contractors actually bid.

A defined, field-informed workflow for turning plans, specifications, revisions, quantities, and open questions into a package the contractor can evaluate.

Scope and deliverables are confirmed in writing before work starts.

Service architecture

Focused support at the points where bids lose clarity.

Engagements can include one focused service or a coordinated package. The objective is not to replace contractor judgment—it is to make that judgment better informed.

01 / REVIEW

Bid Package Review

Structured review of the documents controlling the bid, organized around omissions, revisions, conflicts, and decisions.

  • Drawing, specification, and addenda inventory
  • Bid form and schedule review
  • Priority-page and trade-scope routing
  • Missing-information register
02 / QUANTIFY

Takeoff Organization

Auditable quantity summaries that preserve how a number was measured and what information supported it.

  • Measured quantities and counts
  • Drawing and detail references
  • Verified, assumed, and pending status
  • Revision-aware working backup
03 / EXPOSE

Scope-Gap Analysis

Review of the boundaries and interfaces that can move cost, labor, schedule, or responsibility.

  • Trade ownership and interface points
  • Access, shutdown, and temporary work
  • Testing, disposal, restoration, and closeout
  • Inclusion, exclusion, and clarification notes
04 / SUPPORT

Estimating Support

Organization of labor, equipment, material, crew, productivity, and cost assumptions using the contractor’s agreed pricing basis.

  • Pricing-input and quote registers
  • Crew and productivity assumptions
  • Equipment and logistics inputs
  • Open-cost and allowance tracking
05 / CLARIFY

RFI & Clarification Support

Draft questions tied to a specific source, conflict, missing detail, schedule condition, or field-risk trigger. The contractor reviews, approves, and submits all external communications.

  • Drawing-to-specification conflict notes
  • Missing elevation, material, access, or responsibility questions
  • Decision deadlines and pricing impacts
06 / COORDINATE

ACC 3D Model Review & Coordination

Independent, field-informed review of client-authorized Autodesk Construction Cloud model sets, focused on model control, clash triage, constructability, issue evidence, and decision routing.

  • Model and version register
  • Prioritized clash and coordination issue log
  • Marked views, source cross-checks, and clarification drafts
